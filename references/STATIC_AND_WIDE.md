# Static and wide — beta (v1.3)

Does not replace the law. Use when the take has no second body, or the camera has to move through a place.

Read `SKILL.md` then `Flux_Attention_Disclosure_v1.md` §§12–13 first.

---

## When the main skill is the wrong shape

The main skill's examples are grabs, pins, rims, folds. Those need a binding verb between two bodies.

A portrait, a standing figure in a room, or a wide street does not. If you force `pinned` / `gripped` onto those takes, the compiler invents a second body or a fight.

Do not overload the main skill with more action verbs. Switch bind-type.

| Take | Bind is | Bound thing |
|---|---|---|
| Two bodies | physical verb | the second body, after `Then` |
| Solo / portrait | state-of-being or spatial verb | the place, or the pose already finished |
| Wide / market / street | spatial verb, then optional pan | place surface, then a new unfinished subject |

---

## State-of-being verbs (solo / atmospheric)

Use these as the bind when nobody else is in frame:

stands in, sits at, leans on, waits under, is planted in, holds the doorway, is swallowed by, occupies the bench, fills the mirror

Not: pinned, gripped, crushed, mounting, holding him.

Pattern:

```
LANDING: camera + the body the lens hits
FINISH: that body only (wear as state, sexed surface if visible)
BIND: spatial / stative verb
RESOLVE: the place as a surface (what the light is doing)
FORWARD: grade. Adult.
```

Hold:

> Tight three-quarter in the lofi apartment. The lean athletic adult Caucasian kemonomimi sits on the windowsill — [finish the body] — then the rainy glass takes the rest of the frame, city neon broken across the drops. Adult.

Fail:

> Cozy cluttered bedroom, stuffed bears, disco ball, dual monitors, neon signage, curtains, clothes on the floor. A kemonomimi stands there.

That is a room inventory with a body taped on. Land the body. Let the room arrive as the bind.

## Wide shots and pan

The camera may move. A finished subject may not.

- Finish the landing figure completely.
- `Then` the place, or `Then` a new figure the gaze has just reached.
- Do not return to add a hat, a tail, a second expression, or a missing cock to someone you already closed.
- Crowd is not a subject. One extra finished body, or none. Name the market as canvas, steam, wet asphalt — not as fifty nouns.

Hold:

> High angle on the loading dock. The thick powerful adult male is already at the pallet — [finish him] — then the corrugated wall takes the depth, one sodium bulb in the rafters. Adult.

Fail:

> …finish him… then the dock, then go back and give him the gold ring you forgot.

If the ring matters, it belonged in the finish. Rewrite from landing.

## What not to put in this beta

Species-prior, names-as-tokens, end-hold, negation-content stay in the main law. A static take still cannot say `anthro`, still cannot restamp the tail, still cannot open on a proper name.

This file only changes what counts as a bind, and what "forward" means when the lens moves.
